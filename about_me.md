My name is **Brenno Ferreira** and I am a **phD student** at **Univeristé Libre de Bruxelles (ULB)** in Brussels. I did all my academic path at ULB. It started with a *bachelor's degree* in *Engineering Sciences* in *2023*, followed by a *master's degree* in *Computer Science Engineering* in 2025 with specialization in embedded systems design, and now a phD.

Despite my specialization, my master's thesis was done in the imaging domain at the *Laboratories of Image Synthesis and Analysis (LISA)*. More specifically, I worked at the Virtual Reality department on prototyping a lenticular 3D display. The title of my work was *"3D without Glasses: Prototyping a Lenticular Display"* and the goal was that the final prototype could provide natural 3D perception without visual discomfort, full parallax, full colors and a smooth angular experience. The goal was successfully achieved despite some technical limitation.

After my master's, I was offered a role as **phD student and researcher fellow** within the LISA team that I gladly accepted. My phD's subject is a continuation of my master's thesis: *work on the development and fabrication of 3D displays, finding ways to enhance its quality and its 3D experience*.

You can find my personal page in the [LISA Website](http://lisa.ulb.ac.be/FrBrenno ).
